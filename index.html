What we do

Care that meets urgency with compassion.

We work to reduce infant mortality and long-term disability caused by prematurity through awareness, advocacy, emergency support and collaboration.

Awareness

Knowledge is part of care.

We promote understanding of prematurity among parents, caregivers, healthcare workers and the public, especially in resource-limited settings.
